# Ask one material question with evidence

**Applies when:** reaching a decision that needs owner sign-off.

State the missing decision, evidence, tradeoff, and recommendation. Ask one question at a time while continuing independent authorized work.

**Why:** A narrow question lets the owner decide without reconstructing the whole investigation.

**Example:** `skills/uncomplect/SKILL.md` requires preserved behavior, deleted complexity, the fence, and one material question in its output.

**Held by:** `uncomplect`, Output; review.
